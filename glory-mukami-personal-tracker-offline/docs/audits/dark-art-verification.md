# Dark Art Verification

## Contents right-page cascade check — 25 August 2026

The initial dark Contents surface did not render because an earlier `:last-child` spread background rule had greater selector specificity. The new Contents override includes the same structural page position plus an additional Contents view constraint, so it wins without changing any React markup or navigation behavior.

The requested new art generation requests were unavailable; their failed placeholders were immediately removed. Existing original paintings now provide the visible artwork, with intentionally dark composition layers supplying the requested light-typography contrast.

## Tonal benchmark validation — 25 August 2026

Desktop and phone checks of Workout Studio, Finances, Learning, and Contents confirm the revised composition treatment keeps berry, plum, navy, teal, and smoky-blue brushwork visible. The working pages no longer read as near-black, while their pale editorial and functional typography remains legible.
