## Tonal benchmark

Every new page painting must follow the visible, saturated painterly balance of the existing home and Contents artwork: **plum and berry shadows, layered indigo and cobalt, peacock/deep teal brushwork, occasional warm-gold highlights, and dimensional smoky transitions**. Near-black may appear only as a localized shadow or typography-safe field; it must never dominate the entire painting.

The Contents page is also the required **detail-density benchmark**: each new scene needs multiple visible layers of expressive brushwork, secondary environmental forms, subtle accent details, and a composed focal area. It must not reduce to a sparse gradient or a simple dark field; the typography-safe area should be deliberately quieter, not blank.
